# Code of Conduct

Pick Your AI is an open research and planning project. We want it to be a place where people of any background and experience level can contribute, question, and disagree productively.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas, evidence and code, not people.
- - Be precise and honest about sources. Mark claims as fact, estimate, assumption or unverified, and correct mistakes openly.
  - - Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
    - - Accept review feedback graciously, and give it kindly.
     
      - Not acceptable:
     
      - - Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about someone's identity, background or ability.
        - - Sexualized language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
          - - Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
            - - Sustained disruption of discussions, spam, or deliberately misleading contributions.
              - - Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.
               
                - ## Scope
               
                - This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, the Project board and any other space of the Pick-Your-AI organization, and to people representing the project in public.
               
                - ## Reporting
               
                - Report concerns privately to the organization owners by using GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or by contacting an owner of the Pick-Your-AI organization through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled by owners who are not involved in the incident where possible, kept confidential as far as practical, and reviewed promptly.
               
                - ## Enforcement
               
                - Owners may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently block people from the organization's spaces. We will explain the decision to the people involved where we can.
               
                - ## Changes
               
                - Owners may update this document. Proposed changes go through a pull request.
